Making games for my mom

I'm making games for my mom so she can be more confident using technology. She was really struggling with her first MacBook and some Mac-specific Ul and mechanics. So I made a window management "game". She can even switch to Korean if the English instructions are too confusing.

Mom's MacBook classroom is where I can drop a "lesson" for anything now. Airdrop, password managers, and troubleshooting wifi problems are all "games" that are in the queue.

There's a sentiment I see repeated that goes "Millenials are the only generation that has to teach our parents AND our kids how to use a printer". I'm 2,000 miles away, my Korean language skills aren't good enough to explain concepts like "data retention policy" or "double click to highlight the word". This is bridging our communication gap better anything else I've tried. dthoma1.github.io/mom-games/

Despite the cutesy (and very generic tailwind css) look, for me, increasing my parents' fluency with technology is very serious. Scams and phishing attempts are getting more sophisticated and they love targeting trusting and unsophisticated seniors. Stoking my mom's curiousity in learning about gizmos, software, and internet culture will keep her independent and safer for as long as possible.
